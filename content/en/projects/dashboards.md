## Why I built it

Bring recurring investment observations into pages that can be revisited without collecting and aligning the same information each time.

## What it shows

- Index allocation and recurring-investment observations.
- A-share short-term money flow.
- Published snapshots from scheduled jobs, rather than a real-time market terminal.

## How it works

Python jobs organize the data, SQLite stores related records, and public read-only dashboards display the snapshots. The public frontend is currently hosted on Vercel.
